About Us

The export division of Baidyanath Ayurveda Naini.

“Backed by the pioneers of Ayurveda since 1917.”

Headed by Mr. Dhananjay Sharma, President — Baidyanath

Nitya Naturals Private Limited is a private labeling and contract manufacturing company, and the export division of Baidyanath Ayurveda Naini. For the last fifteen years we have manufactured herbal dietary supplements for brand owners across the world.

01

One-stop manufacturing

Formulation, production, packaging, labeling and export logistics under a single roof — so your team can concentrate on building the brand instead of chasing vendors.

02

Compliance built in

Our GMP certified facility runs modern machinery, and the staff is trained for compliance of CGMP enforced by the US FDA covering 21 CFR 211, 21 CFR 210, 21 CFR 820, ICH Q7 and ISO 9001:2008.

03

Your requirement first

To get started we first need to know your requirements and how we can fulfil them — choose from our existing products and dosage forms, or let us custom-create a product for your market.

Dosage Forms

Six dosage forms, one production line.

Nitya Naturals can produce a variety of nutraceutical product forms that meet the needs of your target market and end consumer.

01

Capsules

Vegetarian and gelatin capsules, hatch-marked and branded, in blister or bottle packs.

02

Tablets

Standard, coated and chewable tablets with weight, hardness and disintegration checks per batch.

03

Syrups

Liquid formulations in food-grade containers, with child-resistant and export-compliant closures.

04

Oils

Herbal and carrier oil blends, filled hygienically with tamper-evident sealing.

05

Creams

Topical cream bases emulsified in GMP vessels and packed in tubes or jars.

06

Pastes

Therapeutic pastes and electuaries, including chyawanprash-style formulations.

Facility

A state-of-the-art Chyawanprash facility.

We have created a state-of-the-art chyawanprash facility with GMP certified machinery, located in the heart of the amla belt at Prayagraj — 2 km from the holy Sangam and 5 km from the ashram of sage Acharya Bharadwaj.
